\section{Budget Subcommittee}
\emph{Effective [DATE]}\\

\subsection{Purpose}
The Budget Subcommittee is established as a standing committee of Council to oversee and authorize expenditures in accordance with this procedure.

\subsection{Composition}
The Budget Subcommittee shall consist of:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The Vice-President, Finance;
    \item The Business Manager;
    \item All members of Council.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Quorum}
A quorum for the Budget Subcommittee is defined as six (6) councillors present, in addition to the Vice-President, Finance and Business Manager (if present).

\subsection{Meetings}
The Budget Subcommittee shall meet a minimum of six (6) times per term.

\subsection{Authority to Approve Expenditures}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The Budget Subcommittee may independently authorize any expenditure that is less than or equal to \$500.
    \item Any expenditure greater than \$500 must be ratified by Council at the next Council meeting following the Subcommittee's approval.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Procedures}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item All expenditure requests presented to the Budget Subcommittee must be accompanied by appropriate documentation and justification.
    \item The Vice-President, Finance shall maintain records of all expenditures approved by the Subcommittee.
    \item The Business Manager shall report on approved expenditures at each Council meeting.
\end{enumerate}
