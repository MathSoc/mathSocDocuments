\section{Code of Conduct}
\emph{effective November 9, 2026; replaces February 18, 2024, November 7, 2023}\\

\subsection{Purpose}
The Mathematics Society recognizes the tremendous work and dedication our student volunteers put into running events, providing services, and overseeing club activities for the student body. As such, this policy expresses our commitment to foster and maintain an environment that is free of harassment and discrimination, for our students to feel safe. The Society will not tolerate anyone intimidating, humiliating, or sabotaging others in our community. The Society aligns with the Federation's \href{https://wusa.ca/document/whistleblower-policy/}{Whistleblower Policy} and is committed to providing and maintaining a professional environment that is based on respect for the dignity and rights of everyone in the Society.

The Society has outlined a code of conduct to highlight our expectations for all individuals who participate in the Mathematics Society, as well as the steps to handle unacceptable behaviour.

The Code of Conduct does not cover criminal matters. Initiating a Code of Conduct complaint does not exclude other paths, such as going to Special Constable Service. For criminal matters, including threats or acts of physical violence, immediately contact Special Constable Service at ext. 22222 or direct line (519) 888-4911.

\subsection{Expected Behaviour}
All members of the Society shall:
\begin{itemize}
	\item exercise consideration and respect to others in speech and actions;
	\item refrain from demeaning, discriminatory, or harassing behaviour and speech; and
	\item be mindful of surroundings and of others.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Unacceptable Behaviour}
Unacceptable behaviour includes intimidating, harassing, aggressive, abusive, discriminatory, or derogatory speech or actions in any location covered by the scope of this Code of Conduct, as defined in section 9, \textit{Scope}.

For the purposes of this procedure, terms such as ``harassment'' and ``discrimination'' are clearly defined in the \href{https://uwaterloo.ca/secretariat/policies-procedures-guidelines/policy-33}{University's Policy 33 - Ethical Behaviour}.

\subsection{Enforcement}
This Code of Conduct is to be enforced by the following Reporting Officers to the best of their ability:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Executive Officers;
	\item Club Executives;
	\item Event organizers; 
	\item Office Managers;
	\item the Chair of the Board; and
	\item the Business Manager.
\end{itemize}
If the Reporting Officer witnesses unacceptable behaviour, they may impose sanctions in accordance with section 6, \textit{Consequences for Unacceptable Behaviour}, A or B, without first going through a committee, unless they are also involved in the situation in question.

\subsection{Experiencing Unacceptable Behaviour}
If a dangerous situation arises or someone is in distress, immediately contact Special Constable Service at ext. 22222 or direct line (519) 888-4911, or call 911.

If there is a violation of this Code of Conduct, contact the nearest eligible Reporting Officer. Complaints received are to be handled by Reporting Officers who are not involved in the situation or closely related to individual(s) in the complaint.

The Reporting Officer will first interview the subject of the complaint and any witnesses, then consult with the Chair of the Board (or replacement; see below) if further action is required and, if necessary, a committee shall be formed to discuss the complaint. 

For all complaints that proceed beyond an initial verbal warning, the Reporting Officer shall complete a Misconduct Reporting Document. The document shall record the date of the complaint, the parties involved, a summary of the allegations, relevant evidence, investigative actions taken, findings, sanctions imposed, and the outcome of any appeal. The Business Manager shall retain these records in a secure manner in accordance with Section 8, Confidentiality.

Individuals who are involved or closely related to those involved (as determined by the rest of the committee) may not sit on this committee. The committee shall consist of:
\begin{itemize}
	\item the Reporting Officer;
	\item the Chair of the Board;
	\item two Mathematics Society Executive Officers;
	\item the Business Manager; and
	\item the Executive Director of the Federation, or designate.
\end{itemize}
The Chair of the Board shall chair this committee; if they are ineligible, a suitable replacement shall be appointed by the committee. The committee is appointed on a per-incident basis. The chair of the committee shall be familiar with the Federation's Whistleblower Policy, University Policy 33, and the Federation's MoU with Societies, and they shall notify the Federation, providing a copy of Appendix A, when a temporary or 1-year ban has been imposed on any individual.

\subsubsection{Investigation Process and Timelines}
The Investigation and Decision Process is outlined as below:
\begin{enumerate}
	\item Upon receipt of a complaint, the Reporting Officer shall acknowledge receipt within five (5) business days.
	\item The Reporting Officer or committee shall conduct a reasonable investigation, which may include interviews with the complainant, respondent, and relevant witnesses.
	\item The subject of the complaint shall be informed of the allegations and provided an opportunity to respond before a final decision is made.
	\item A decision shall normally be rendered within twenty (20) business days of receiving the complaint. Where this timeline cannot reasonably be met, the parties shall be notified of the delay.
	\item All decisions and sanctions shall be communicated in writing to the subject of the complaint.
	\item A written decision shall include: 
		\begin{itemize}
			\item a summary of the complaint;
			\item findings made during the investigation;
			\item any sanctions imposed;
			\item the duration of sanctions, if applicable; and
			\item information regarding the appeals process.
		\end{itemize}
	\item The Business Manager shall securely retain records of complaints, investigations, sanctions, and appeals for a minimum of five (5) years, after which records shall be destroyed in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Consequences of Unacceptable Behaviour}
In determining an appropriate sanction, the Reporting Officer or committee shall consider Appendix B, the severity of the conduct, prior incidents, the impact on affected individuals, and any mitigating circumstances.

Any individual found to be in violation of this Code of Conduct shall receive at least one consequence of the following list, based on what the committee or the Reporting Officer deems appropriate:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.]
	\item a verbal or written warning;
	\item a request to leave the area for the rest of the event or the day, whichever is appropriate;
	\item an immediate ban from an area/space while investigation is being conducted by the committee;
	\item a temporary ban from specific Mathematics Society spaces, events, communications channels, or activities, or from all such areas, as deemed appropriate by the committee. The scope of the ban shall be proportionate to the nature and severity of the incident;
	\item a 1-year ban from specific Mathematics Society spaces, events, communications channels, or activities, or from all such areas, in the event of a serious incident or repeated violations. The scope of the ban shall be proportionate to the nature and severity of the incident; or
	\item filing a formal complaint to the Executive Director of the Federation.
\end{enumerate}
If the situation is handled by the Reporting Officer, they may assign either of consequence A or B. If the Reporting Officer deems that a more severe sanction is appropriate, they may contact the Chair of the Board to pursue further action. If the situation is handled by the committee, then the committee may assign any sanction on the above list, as they deem appropriate, with consultation from the Federation. For greater clarity, a Club Executive would be considered a Reporting Officer and may assign either of consequence A or B.  Any further consequences from the list would need to be handled through the committee.

If an individual who is not permitted to be in an area governed by this Code of Conduct is found in such an area, then any Reporting Officer that is aware of this is to ask the individual to leave. If they refuse, the Reporting Officer is to contact Special Constables and have the individual removed. 

In the event that an individual is banned for a term they have already paid fees for, they may opt out of those fees as per the Mathematics Society refund request requirements.

\subsection{Grievances}
If a sanctioned individual disagrees with the consequences assigned, they may appeal the decision within ten (10) business days of receiving the written decision by submitting an appeal to the Executive Director of WUSA, or designate.

Upon receipt of an appeal, the Executive Director of WUSA, or designate, shall establish an Appeals Committee. Individuals who participated in the original investigation, decision, or sanctioning process shall not serve on this committee.  The Appeals Committee will be comprised of the following members:
\begin{itemize}
	\item two Directors appointed by the Board;
	\item one Mathematics Society Executive Officer not previously involved;
	\item the Executive Director of WUSA, or designate;
	\item one additional student representative appointed by WUSA.
\end{itemize}
The new committee shall appoint a chair to oversee the process and determine whether to uphold, change, or remove the consequences assigned. Information from the initial investigation will need to be disclosed to the new committee.  The final decision from the Appeals Committee shall be presented to the sanctioned individual and shared with the Chair of the Board of Directors.

A one-year ban shall automatically expire at the conclusion of its stated duration unless an extension has been imposed pursuant to this procedure. The Business Manager shall maintain records sufficient to track sanction expiry dates and shall notify relevant Reporting Officers when a sanction has expired.

Where an individual subject to a one-year ban has violated the terms of the sanction or has been the subject of additional substantiated complaints during the sanction period, the committee may impose an extension of up to one academic term.  Any such extension must be communicated to the individual in writing before the original expiry date.

\subsection{Confidentiality}
The Mathematics Society recognizes that all members have a right to privacy, and will handle complaints confidentially. As such, proceedings will be kept confidential as described below:
\begin{itemize}
	\item information will only be reported to Special Constable Service when directly required;
	\item information will be kept in the Mathematics Society Business Manager's records and be available to Executive Officers, as outlined in Appendix A; and
	\item possible Reporting Officers will be informed of the sanction on the individual in question as is deemed necessary to enforce the sanction (e.g. at events), but no details of the incident will be disclosed.
	\item the misconduct reporting document shall be shared with the Associate Dean of Undergraduate Studies of the Faculty.  Student names shall be redacted for confidentiality.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Scope}
This procedure applies to all spaces monitored by the Mathematics Society and the individuals using that space, the aforementioned spaces including but not limited to the main office, Coffee and Donut shop, Comfy Lounge, club rooms, and public spaces that have been booked for MathSoc's use. This policy also applies to any and all communication created for Mathematics Society use, including but not limited to the Mathematics Society digital space and any chat created for office staff and clubs, as well as during any Mathematics Society event.

For greater clarity, sanctions issued under this procedure may only restrict participation in Mathematics Society governed events, activities, digital spaces, offices, and facilities while such spaces are being operated, managed, or booked by the Mathematics Society. This procedure does not authorize restrictions on attendance at events organized by independent student organizations, the University, or WUSA unless otherwise authorized by those entities.
