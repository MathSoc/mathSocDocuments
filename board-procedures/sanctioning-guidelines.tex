\section{Sanctioning Guidelines}

The sanctions listed in Appendix B are provided as guidance only and are intended to promote consistency in the application of this Code of Conduct. Each complaint shall be assessed on its own merits, and the Reporting Officer or committee retains discretion to impose any sanction authorized under Section 6 that it deems appropriate in the circumstances. Where required under this procedure, sanctions shall be imposed in consultation and collaboration with the Federation, including the Executive Director or designate.

\begin{center}
	\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{| X | X |}
		\hline
		\rowcolor{lightgray} \textbf{Violation} & \textbf{Typical Sanction}  \\ 
		\hline
		Minor disruptive conduct & Verbal warning \\  
		\hline
		Repated disruptive conduct & Removal from event and/or space \\
		\hline
		Harassment or discriminatory comments & Event and/or space removal, temporary ban \\
		\hline 
		Harassment with significant impact & Term ban \\
		\hline
		Threats, intimidation, repeated harassment & Depending on severity, and in consultation with the Federation, referral to Special Constable Services (on-campus) or WRPS (off-campus). Sanction dependent on above direction \\
		\hline
		Physical violence or credible threats & Referral to Special Constable Services (on-campus) or WRPS (off-campus). In consultation with the Federation, sanction dependent on above direction \\
		\hline
	\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
